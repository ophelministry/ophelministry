Our Origin Story / ஊழிய தரிசன பின்னணி

Founded in the year 2010 in our hometown of Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, Ophel Ministry began as a humble fellowship circle dedicated to mutual intercession and burden-bearing. What started with a small group of faithful believers meeting weekly has steadily grown into a wide reaching spiritual anchor network.

Throughout our early mission years, we focused deeply on traveling outreach, family crisis counseling, and neighborhood deliverance operations. Over more than a decade of active labor, our core message remains completely unchanged: constructing an open sanctuary of unceasing prayer, deep healing reconciliation, and global spiritual encouragement for all people.
